# Familie Bán & Kagol – werkwijze

Genealogy research and a static family site (Dutch) for the Bán and Kagol (Kargol/Kargul) families, Hungary.
Same way of working as C:\Users\thijn\hillen-familie (see its CLAUDE.md): only certain links, contradictions noted
not silently fixed, research notes per topic in `onderzoek/`, updated and committed at the end of every session.
Living people are never named on the site (the daughter of András Kagol and Mária Fridlik stays "dochter").

## Site
- `index.html` (story), `stamboom.html` + `js/stamboom.js` (D3 ancestor tree per couple), `kaart.html` (Leaflet map),
  `bronnen.html` (sources and open questions), `css/style.css`. Test via `python -m http.server`, not file://.
- `data/familie.json` is generated by `python scripts/build_data.py` – edit the script, not the JSON.
  New couple: add to `C` with `parents` = [husband's parents couple, wife's parents couple].
- `data/plaatsen.json`: coordinates (Nominatim, 1 req/s). A new place needs an entry here to appear on the map.
- Photos in `assets/img/` from Wikimedia Commons, small (max 900 px); licence and author in the caption.

## Research notes (`onderzoek/`)
- `myheritage.md`: the family's MyHeritage tree (Hillen Family Tree), what is visible and how to read it.
- `familysearch.md`: all FamilySearch records and Family Tree persons (ids), plus open leads and dead ends.

## Scripts (debug Chrome on port 9223, user logged in to FamilySearch and MyHeritage)
- `fs.py OUT "query"` record search (saves text + `.links`), `fsparse.py OUT` compact result list.
- `rec.py ID…` open records (`rec_ID.txt`), `rp.py ID…` print them. `tree.py PID…` FamilySearch tree person.
- `root.py ID…` MyHeritage tree panel per person. `go.py`, `lees_open_tabblad.py`, `mnls.py` (MNL database search).
- `commons_zoek.py` / `commons_info.py`: Wikimedia Commons photos with licence.
